Legal

Licence, privacy and terms

Wobu is free, open source, runs no servers and collects nothing about you. These are the documents that say all of that properly. They come with the app too — inside every installer, and readable under Settings › Legal.

Documents

  • Privacy policy

    Everywhere your work can possibly go, and what is sent when it does. What stays on your disk, where it sits, and how your keys are kept.

  • Terms of use

    What you are allowed to do with Wobu, the fact that it comes with no warranty, and why who owns a generated picture is between you and the AI service, not us.

  • MIT licence

    The licence Wobu is given to you under, in full. Short, and unusually readable.

Wobu is built on a lot of other people's free software. They are all credited in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md, which comes with the app and is shown under Settings › Licences.

wobu

Wobu runs no servers. There is no account, no tracking and no update check. The AI work happens on your own machine, or on your own account with a service you chose.
