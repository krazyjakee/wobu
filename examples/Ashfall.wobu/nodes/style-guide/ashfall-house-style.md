## Notes

medium: gouache + ink line, kept loose
never fully rendered — leave brush marks
light is always volumetric, ash in the air
palette stays muted; one warm accent per image only

## Description

### Medium

Gouache and ink on toned paper. Economical brushwork; edges left unresolved outside the focal zone.

### Lighting model

A single warm ash-lit key, always volumetric. Cold sky bounce fills the shadow side.

### Palette

- #2b2118
- #c2703a
- #7fa5a3
- #8b8f7a
- #e6d7bc

### Never

- Glossy 3D render
- Neon or fluorescent hues
- Lens flare, chromatic aberration
