## Description

### Costume

Quilted ash-canvas coat, knee length, worn open. A single ceramic shoulder plate on the working side. Burnt-orange lining is the only permitted colour.

### Iconography

The signet — a brass disc struck with a five-vent mark — is fused directly to the collarbone during induction and cannot be removed, only defaced.

### Never

- Bright dyes outside the lining
- Matched pairs of shoulder plates
