## Description

### Era

Forty years after the eruption. Iron-age craft supplemented by scavenged pre-fall glass and brass.

### Tone

Weary and practical rather than grim. People have adapted; the world is worn, not ruined.

### Materials

Ash-glazed ceramic, oiled leather and ash-canvas, salvaged green glass, cold-forged iron.

### Never

- Gunpowder or firearms
- Plastics or synthetics
