port town built on the old pier stacks

## Description

### Architecture

Built upward on the old pier stacks — stacked timber and salvaged glass, tied rather than nailed.

### Ambient light

Low, grey-green and diffuse. Contrast stays compressed; wet surfaces do the work.

### Wear & age

Salt bloom on every metal surface; rope and net strung between levels as older lines are left to rot in place.
