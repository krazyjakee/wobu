## Notes

egg tempera, thin glazes, tooth of the panel left showing
no direct sun anywhere — the sky is the light source
palette stays cold, the one warm note is lamplight

## Description

### Medium

Egg tempera on gessoed panel, built in thin glazes.

### Lighting model

Flat sea-light from a white sky, doubled by the water under it.

### Palette

- #8b8f7a
- #4a554e

### Never

- Direct sunlight
- Photographic detail
